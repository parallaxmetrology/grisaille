    Repair

    How this is read

    The picture is squinted exactly as the plan was (CIE L*, Kuwahara flattening), then each pixel is compared with the plan. Exact: it falls in the plan's own value band. Pattern: it falls in the right band after one global key and contrast adjustment fitted to the whole picture, so a picture that went lighter, darker or flatter overall can still hold the pattern; the key shift and contrast are reported beside it. Neighbouring values closer than 3 L* in the picture read as merged. A plan shape is lost when under half of it holds its value. These are counts of pixels and differences in L*, not grades.

    Repair pushes the lightness of the chosen area back to the plan (as the picture's own key and contrast put it), keeps the colour, then repaints only that area with the generator held to the corrected picture. Outside the area nothing changes. The repaired picture gets its own receipt.

    Plan changes: with a picture selected, edit the plan in card 1 (move a dark, widen a band, merge two values). Only the pixels you changed get their lightness moved to the new plan, and only they and a 24 px edge are repainted, so an object the change cuts through is repainted whole. The receipt reads the picture against the new plan before and after.
